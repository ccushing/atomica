
I want to make a 2d shooter in the style of SubSpace (also known as Continuum). I had created a working version years ago in Microsoft's XNA framework, but that is no longer supported. Here are the feautures of the game:

1. 2D shooter where the user flies around in a spaceship in a large, 2D map.
2. The map can be loaded via a .csv file which defines start points and walls.
3. Controls are : 
	Left Arrow = Rotate Left
	Right Arrow = Rotate Right
	Up Arrow = Forward Thrust
	Down Arrow = Reverse Thrust
	Space Bar = Fire
4. The ship should follow realistic physics of a ship flying in space.
5. The ship bounces off walls without damage.
6. There is a radar box in the lower right corner that shows the map, player position and enemy positions
7. Different Weapons
	Normal - Bullets fire from ship and do not bounce with full damage.
	Bouncing - Bullet bounce off of walls, but with 1/2 damage.
